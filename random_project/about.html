1. Fruits

Nutritional Content: Rich in vitamins (like Vitamin C, A, and K), fiber, antioxidants, and water. Examples: Apples, bananas, oranges, berries, grapes. Benefits: Boost immunity, promote heart health, aid in digestion, and are low in calories.

2. Vegetables

Nutritional Content: High in fiber, vitamins (A, C, K), minerals (like potassium, magnesium), and antioxidants. Examples: Spinach, broccoli, carrots, bell peppers, and cauliflower. Benefits: Support immune function, improve digestion, help in weight management, and reduce chronic disease risks.

3. Whole Grains

Nutritional Content: Good source of complex carbohydrates, fiber, B vitamins, and minerals such as iron and magnesium. Examples: Brown rice, quinoa, oats, whole wheat, barley. Benefits: Aid in digestion, regulate blood sugar, lower cholesterol, and provide lasting energy.
